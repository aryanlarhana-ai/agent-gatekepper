# Business Brief

## Business and industry
Amazon India order and refund operations, with an academic focus on autonomous agent governance.

## Problems
1. Orders with missing identity verification may be approved or fulfilled incorrectly.
2. High-value orders require step-up human review.
3. Repeat refund activity may indicate abuse or fraud.
4. Out-of-stock orders create avoidable cancellations and customer dissatisfaction.
5. Financial and operational priorities can conflict.

## Decision maker
Amazon India operations or risk manager.

## Decisions
- Block, escalate or allow an order or refund.
- Identify repeated-refund customers for review.
- Stop fulfillment where stock is unavailable.
- Approve or reject escalated recommendations.

## Data required
Order ID, customer ID, product, order amount, ID-proof status, refund request, prior refunds and stock availability.
